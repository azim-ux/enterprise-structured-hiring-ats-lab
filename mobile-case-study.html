Risk and operating discipline

Human judgment remains reviewable.

  • Halo-effect review: a +0.68 subjective-versus-composite gap flags CAND-2026-0013 for review without diagnosing intent.
  • Structured rubrics: behaviorally anchored questions, common probes, and scoring notes reduce uncontrolled variance.
  • Role-based access: recruiters, interviewers, hiring managers, HRIS administrators, and auditors receive separated permissions.
  • Reason codes: dispositions and overrides require documented, job-related evidence.
  • Privacy: synthetic public data, limited fields, retention rules, and demographic separation support safe demonstration.
  • UAT: stage transitions, permissions, formulas, exception paths, and reporting outputs have explicit acceptance checks.
Built deliverables: source datasets, data dictionary, ATS workflow and RACI, rubrics, validity model, fairness matrix, privacy matrix, UAT register, dashboard, and executive deck.

Executive takeaway

Scale works when evidence, workflow, and accountability move together.

What leaders can decide

Where the funnel loses viable candidates, which requisitions miss service levels, and where evidence or capacity needs intervention.

What operators can run

A five-requisition workflow with clear owners, structured interviews, auditable stage gates, and paginated candidate operations.

What HRIS teams can govern

Entity relationships, permissions, reason codes, data retention, formulas, and acceptance criteria.

What the model does not claim

No causal proof, no automated employment decision, no production deployment, and no real candidate data.

Research anchor: Sackett et al. (2022) selection-method meta-analysis, applied as an evidence foundation rather than a claim of local predictive validity.
