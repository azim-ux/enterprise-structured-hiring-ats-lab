# Requisitions and Roles

## Organization and workforce context

Apex Precision Dynamics Ltd. is a fictional 70-person precision-manufacturing firm in Aligarh, India. The 2026 simulation models five high-volume requisition families spanning grades G1–G4. Compensation ranges are synthetic annual fixed cash in Indian rupees, exclude statutory benefits and variable pay, and require market and legal review before real use.

## REQ-2026-ENG-G4 — Senior Precision Engineer

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Department / grade | Engineering / APD-G4 |
| Target / applicant volume | 10 hires / 800 applicants |
| Synthetic range | ₹1,150,000–₹1,450,000 |
| Purpose | Lead manufacturability, root-cause analysis, capability improvement, and safe technical change |
| Critical outcomes | Reduce repeat defects; improve first-pass yield; govern engineering changes; coach technical teams |

**Required:** engineering degree or equivalent demonstrated expertise; precision-manufacturing problem solving; statistical process-control literacy; controlled root-cause methods; risk assessment; evidence-based communication.

**Preferred:** CNC or metrology exposure; capability studies; supplier-quality collaboration; coaching experience.

**Assessment:** a two-hour tolerance-drift work sample, structured interview, and senior-level job-knowledge test. Panel: Engineering Manager, Quality Lead, Operations representative, and trained TA facilitator.

## Common assessment architecture

| Component | Duration | Weight | High-volume control |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Automated knockout screen | Under 5 minutes | Gate only | Versioned job-related questions; no protected data; human appeal |
| Evidence résumé screen | 12 minutes | Gate only | Redacted batch queue; calibrated evidence checklist |
| Structured phone screen | 25 minutes | Gate only | Same questions; scheduled capacity waves |
| Work sample | 90–120 minutes | 40% | Standard brief; secure versions; accommodation route |
| Structured BARS interview | 45–60 minutes | 40% | Four competencies; independent score lock |
| Job-knowledge test | 30–45 minutes | 20% | Blueprint control; item exposure monitoring |
| Calibration | 20 minutes | No new score | Exception-only review; no demographic access |

## Required versus preferred rule

Required criteria must be demonstrably job-related and applied consistently. Preferred criteria can differentiate only after required criteria are met; they cannot silently become knockout gates. Years of experience are not an automatic proxy for skill, especially in the G1 trainee family. Every rejection maps to a controlled reason, rule version, stage event, and candidate communication.

## Capacity and panel controls

Assessment waves have maximum candidate and assessor loads. The ATS prevents double-booking, assigns trained alternates, and monitors assessor severity and SLA performance. No assessor evaluates more than the approved daily limit. Referral source and demographic cohort remain hidden. Conflicts involving reporting lines, family, financial interests, referrals, or close prior working relationships require reassignment.
