# Do-Not-Touch List

The following boundaries remain in force unless a reviewed issue and explicit human approval change them.

- Do not introduce real candidate, employee, recruiter, client, or company data.
- Do not expose phone numbers, personal email addresses, government IDs, credentials, local paths, private documents, or repository secrets.
- Do not use demographic cohort fields in screening, scoring, ranking, interview, offer, or individual decision logic.
- Do not change the governed synthetic KPI contract (4,000 candidates, 5 requisitions, 120 hires, 28.5 days, 91.8% SLA, 0.87 AIR, 40/40/20 weighting) without an issue explaining the evidence and a full reconciliation.
- Do not remove validity, fairness, synthetic-data, non-causal, legal-review, or non-production disclaimers.
- Do not label the system “enterprise-ready,” “production-ready,” legally compliant, bias-free, validated, or secure.
- Do not fabricate issue, branch, commit, test, review, deployment, or release history.
- Do not rewrite published Git history, force-push, merge a pull request, modify repository/Pages settings, create a release, or publish externally without explicit human approval.
- Do not replace the MIT license or remove the Chart.js license/third-party notice.
- Do not silently regenerate or replace the published PDFs; preserve source-to-output traceability and visually verify every page.
- Do not treat the UAT register, RBAC matrix, workflow narrative, or architecture diagram as proof that a runtime control exists.
- Do not begin a later stage while the current stage review gate remains open.
